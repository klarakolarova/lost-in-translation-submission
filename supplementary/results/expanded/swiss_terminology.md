# Swiss parliamentary terminology

Comparison of 598 completed translation pairs from motions classified as French-authored. This checks whether French institutional terms are rendered using the expected Swiss German terminology.

## Institutional terms

Counts refer to texts containing the French term. A German version counts as retaining the term when an accepted form appears anywhere in that text. Alternatives show the first matching replacement; ‘other’ means no listed alternative matched, and ‘lost’ is the rule’s category for an interpellation not retained.

## Abbreviations

A French abbreviation counts as retained only when it appears without its German equivalent.

French abbreviations of Swiss authorities and laws: 119 texts contain one; the machine output keeps the French form in 93, the official translation in 0.

Most frequently retained French abbreviations (French → German; number of texts): LAMal → KVG (14), AVS → AHV (11), CFF → SBB (8), LACI → AVIG (8), OFAG → BLW (6), DETEC → UVEK (6), EPF → ETH (5), LHID → StHG (5).

## Review status

New terminology matches have not been manually checked. Term presence anywhere in a document does not guarantee correct translation of every occurrence. See [screening](translation_screening.md) and [terminology examples](terminology_examples.csv).
