# Swiss parliamentary terminology

Comparison of 259 completed translation pairs from motions classified as French-authored. This checks whether French institutional terms are rendered using the expected Swiss German terminology.

## Institutional terms

Counts refer to texts containing the French term. A German version counts as retaining the term when an accepted form appears anywhere in that text. Alternatives show the first matching replacement; ‘other’ means no listed alternative matched, and ‘lost’ is the rule’s category for an interpellation not retained.

## Abbreviations

A French abbreviation counts as retained only when it appears without its German equivalent.

French abbreviations of Swiss authorities and laws: 54 texts contain one; the machine output keeps the French form in 41, the official translation in 0.

Most frequently retained French abbreviations (French → German; number of texts): AVS → AHV (7), LAMal → KVG (4), OFAG → BLW (4), LPP → BVG (3), EPF → ETH (3), CFF → SBB (3), LHID → StHG (3), DFF → EFD (3).
