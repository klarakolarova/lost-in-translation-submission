# Author language and the sampling pool

This classification identifies the pool of motions likely to have been written in French before translation into German. Author language is estimated from canton and biography information.

## Classification rule

For multilingual cantons, a language was assigned when at least 75% of French/German word-list matches supported it; otherwise, it remained unclassified.

## Agreement check

Among members from monolingual German/French cantons whose biographies could be classified, 465 of 466 biography-based estimates agreed with the canton language. This measures agreement with the canton proxy, rather than verified author language.

## Eligible sampling pool

Of 10,573 eligible French–German text pairs, 2,245 were classified as French-authored and formed the pool for sample selection.

## Data source

Author records retrieved 2026-11-16T09:01:30+00:00; archived in `data/authors/`. Per-motion classifications are saved in [motion_author_language.csv](data/motion_author_language.csv).
